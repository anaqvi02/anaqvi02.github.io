ORIGIN STORY

Why it exists.

I lost to the hardest Knucklebones difficulty in Cult of the Lamb and refused to accept it. So I built the game in Python, built an MCTS bot that plays it, let it self-play thousands of times to understand what good play looks like, and then spent a lot of matches trying to beat it myself. Whether I ever did is between me and the bot.

HOW IT WORKS

The Game & The AI.

A full rules engine for the dice game: 3×3 boards, column scoring where pairs score 2× and triples 3×, and placement that wipes matching dice from the opponent's column. The AI picks moves with Monte Carlo Tree Search plus RAVE, which shares statistics between symmetric placements, tuned by a UCT constant of 1.414 and a RAVE blend of 350, at 5000 iterations per move.

HOW IT WORKS

The Heuristic & Training.

Rollouts are guided by an evaluation that combines score gain, opponent disruption, and combo bonuses, so the search knows the greedy shape of the game before it starts. train.py runs self-play to tune the parameters, play.py drops you into a terminal match, and the rules engine the bot thinks with is the same code that runs the game, so it never cheats by construction.
