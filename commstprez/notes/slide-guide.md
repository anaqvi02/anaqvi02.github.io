# Slide guide: Listening (14 slides)

1. **Waiting:** "Listening. We'll start in a moment." Leave it up while the room settles; click once to begin.
2. **Marvin texts:** read his message. Everyone writes the reply they'd actually send (45 s).
3. **Share what you wrote, and why:** 3 min. Read replies out, then talk about what they were trying to do for Marvin.
4. **What people send Marvin:** the spread of replies. Ask which sound familiar; read a bad one out.
5. **Eight responses:** the replies sort onto the line from listening to fixing. One click names each.
6. **Quote:** advising is the most common response (Adler, p. 164).
7. **The clip:** play it. No talking.
8. **Where he landed:** four clicks: each circle expands, then its exact video quote appears. Analyzing, advising, evaluating, then empathizing. Only empathizing gets a thank you.
9. **Round 1, then takeup:** where does your reply land, and was that the goal? Has someone fixed when you wanted to vent? After the timer, keep it up: hear three replies, Ali says each back, Marvin places it.
10. **Was he wrong?:** show of hands. Advice isn't always bad.
11. **Before giving advice:** is it needed, is it wanted, did you listen first? (Adler, pp. 164–165)
12. **Johnathan texts:** "i bombed the calc midterm. like actually bombed it." Everyone writes a reply (30 s).
13. **Discuss:** is it different from your Marvin reply? Take two out loud and say where each lands. Did more of us start on the left? He didn't ask for a plan.
14. **Thanks:** "Listen first. Fix if they ask."
